Can You Hear It?

Five rounds. Headphones required — that is not a suggestion. 🎧

⚠️
Turn your volume DOWN before you start. These are pure tones. They are louder than they feel, and they do not warn you. Start low. You can raise it in round one.

A binaural beat only works when each ear gets its own tone. Your left ear hears one. Your right ear hears a slightly different one. Your brain hears the gap between them as a slow pulse. 🌀

That pulse is not in the recording. Neither ear gets it. Your head builds it. Take one earbud out and it disappears.

Most people have never checked whether they can actually hear this. Some hear it right away. Some hear almost nothing. Both are normal — and if your headphones are wired backwards, round one will catch it.
