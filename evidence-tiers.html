Three Tiers

Twelve claims about frequency, sound and the Moon. No sources shown. Your job is to place each one on the right tier of evidence — then find out where it actually sits, and why.

This is not a memory test. It is a sorting skill. Once you have it, it works on any claim anybody ever hands you.

🔴  NO EVIDENCEThe claim may be old, popular, or sincerely believed. There is no credible published support for it — or it is simply wrong.
🟢  REAL BUT LIMITEDPublished, peer-reviewed support exists. The effect is modest, the literature is imperfect, and nobody serious is promising miracles.
🌙  REAL AND CONTESTEDGood researchers in good journals measured something — and other good researchers could not reproduce it. The argument is still running.
